# Bölüm 1 – Kayıp Top (≈70 sn, 9 klip)

**Hikâye:** Ada ile Emir parkta kırmızı topla oynar. Sinsi Sami ağacın arkasından süzülüp topu çuvalına atar ve kaçar. Çocuklar üzülür. Polis Burak durumu görür, sirenli arabasıyla peşine düşer. Sami kaçarken su birikintisinde kayıp poposunun üstüne düşer. Burak onu nazikçe kaldırır. Sami topu geri verir ve eğilerek özür diler. Sonunda dördü birlikte top oynar.

Her video promptunun sonuna README'deki **stil cümlesini** ekle.

---

### Klip 1 – Parkta oyun (8 sn)
- **Kullanılacak resim:** önce sahne resmi üret (Ada + Emir resimlerini referans ver)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `Ada (blonde girl in blue overalls with pink bow) and Emir (brown-haired boy in turquoise car T-shirt) playing with a shiny red ball in a colorful playground park, wide shot, happy faces`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The two kids happily kick a red ball back and forth on the grass, ball bounces, they laugh and jump, gentle slow camera push-in`

### Klip 2 – Sinsi Sami gözetliyor (6 sn)
- **Kullanılacak resim:** `Renkli Sokakta Çizgi Film Hırsızı.png`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The masked cartoon thief peeks out from behind a tree, looks left and right with a mischievous grin, then tiptoes forward on his toes very carefully, comedic sneaky walk`

### Klip 3 – Top çalınıyor (8 sn)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `The masked cartoon thief in black coat and hat grabbing a red ball from the grass in a colorful park, sack on his shoulder, kids blurred in background`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The thief quickly scoops up the red ball, stuffs it into his sack, then sneaks away on tiptoes, looking back over his shoulder, funny cartoon motion`

### Klip 4 – Çocuklar üzgün (6 sn)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `Close-up of Ada and Emir looking surprised and sad in the park, Ada pointing to the side, big shiny eyes`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The kids gasp in surprise, their faces turn sad, the girl points toward the thief, the boy rubs his eyes, soft camera move`

### Klip 5 – Polis Burak görev başında (6 sn)
- **Kullanılacak resim:** `Neşeli Çizgi Polis ve Şehir.png`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The friendly police officer uncrosses his arms, notices something, gives a confident nod and salute, then turns and runs toward his blue police car`

### Klip 6 – Sirenli araba (8 sn)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `Cute rounded blue police car with red and blue light bar, the police officer driving inside, on a lavender-gray road in a pastel colorful town, three-quarter front view`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The police car drives forward along the colorful street, red and blue siren lights flashing, camera tracks alongside smoothly, lamp posts and shops pass by`

### Klip 7 – Komik kovalamaca (8 sn)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `The masked cartoon thief running away with a sack on a colorful street, small water puddle on the road ahead, police car far behind`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The thief runs with exaggerated cartoon steps, steps into a puddle, slips and lands softly on his bottom with a splash, the sack drops and the red ball rolls out, funny not scary`

### Klip 8 – Özür (8 sn)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `The police officer gently helping the masked thief stand up on a colorful street, the thief holding a red ball, both cartoon characters, friendly mood`
- **Video promptu:**
  `The officer helps the thief up, the thief looks embarrassed, holds out the red ball, then bows politely to say sorry, the officer smiles and pats his shoulder`

### Klip 9 – Hep birlikte (10 sn)
- **Sahne resmi promptu:**
  `Ada, Emir, the police officer and the now-friendly thief (mask pushed up) playing ball together in a sunny colorful park, colorful confetti in the air`
- **Video promptu:**
  `Everyone laughs and passes the red ball to each other, kids jump with joy, colorful confetti falls, camera slowly pulls back to a wide happy shot`

---

**Sesler (ben ekleyeceğim):** park kuş sesleri, top zıplama, sinsi yürüyüş "tıp tıp" müziği, "eyvah!", polis sireni, şapırt su, düşme "bom", alkış ve konfeti.
